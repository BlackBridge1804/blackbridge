Straight talk

What BlackBridge does, and doesn't

Credit reports are personal, and nobody can promise you a result. Here's exactly what to expect.

What we do

  • Read your report and flag items that may be inaccurate or incomplete
  • Explain each finding in plain English
  • Draft dispute letters for you to review and send
  • Help you track the responses you get back

What we don't do

  • Guarantee that any item will be removed
  • Promise a specific score increase
  • Contact the bureaus or creditors on your behalf
  • Tell you to dispute accurate information
Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is the scan really free?

Yes. Uploading your report and seeing your summary costs nothing. The full breakdown and dispute letters unlock after a one-time report fee.

Where do I get my credit report?

You can get free reports from Equifax, Experian and TransUnion at annualcreditreport.com, the site federal law set up for this. Save the report as a PDF and upload it here.

Will you remove items from my credit report?

No one can promise that. Bureaus and creditors decide what changes after you dispute. We help you find items worth a closer look and prepare your letters. What happens next is up to them.

Can I dispute items myself for free?

Yes. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information with the credit bureaus yourself, at no cost, without using any company. BlackBridge is a tool that saves you the work of finding the issues and writing the letters.

Do I have to send the letters myself?

Yes. We prepare them and you review, sign and mail them. That way you know exactly what's being sent in your name.

Which bureaus does it work with?

Upload one report at a time. If you want all three bureaus checked, upload each one and you'll get findings and letters for each.
