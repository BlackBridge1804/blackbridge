Refund Policy

When you can get your money back.

Last updated: October 23, 2026. We want you to be comfortable buying from us. Here is exactly when you can get your money back.

1. Three-business-day refund

You can cancel your purchase and get a full refund within 3 business days of paying, for any reason. Business days are Monday through Friday, not counting federal holidays. Just email BlackBridgeoffice@Gmail.com with the email address on your account and tell us you want to cancel.

2. If something goes wrong on our side

You will get a full refund, at any time, if:

  • we are unable to read your report or finish your scan, or
  • your full breakdown or letters fail to generate because of a problem with our Service that we cannot fix, or
  • you were charged more than once for the same report.

3. After the first 3 business days

Once you have received your full breakdown and letters and the 3-business-day window has passed, the fee is generally not refundable, unless the law requires it or we agree to it. Please note that we cannot control what a credit bureau, creditor, or collector decides after you send a dispute. How a dispute turns out is not, on its own, a reason for a refund. We also never promise any particular result.

4. How refunds are paid

We refund to the same payment method you used. Once we approve a refund, we process it within 5 business days. Your bank or card company may take several more days to show it. If you paid by a direct method like Zelle or a bank transfer, we will send the refund back to the same account.

5. Problem with a charge?

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. Most issues can be fixed quickly: email BlackBridgeoffice@Gmail.com or call (669) 272-2854.

6. Done-for-you services

If you sign up for a service where we file disputes for you, your cancellation and refund rights are set out in the written agreement and notice you will receive before work starts.

7. Contact

Refund questions? Email BlackBridgeoffice@Gmail.com or call (669) 272-2854.
