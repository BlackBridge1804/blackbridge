Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Last updated: October 23, 2026. This policy explains what information BlackBridge LLC collects through BlackBridge, how we use it, and the choices you have. Credit reports are sensitive, and we treat them that way.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored in protected form).
  • Mailing address and phone number: the return address you give us so your dispute letters are complete. Phone is optional.
  • Credit report files and the information in them: the report you upload, including account names, balances, dates, and payment history, and anything else that appears in it.
  • What you add inside the Service: outcomes you record, notes, and details you enter for special disputes (for example, an identity theft report number).
  • Payment information: we do not store your card or bank account numbers. Payments are handled by our payment provider, and we receive confirmation that a payment was made.
  • Basic technical data: things like your IP address, browser type, and server logs, used to keep the Service running and secure.
  • Messages to us: anything you send by email or phone.

2. How we use it

  • To read your report, find items that may be inaccurate or incomplete, and draft your letters.
  • To run your account, take payment, and unlock your report.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To keep the Service secure and to prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To improve the Service, for example by looking at which features are used and fixing errors.
  • To follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your credit report to show you ads.

3. Automated processing and AI

To read the reports you upload, we send the report's text to an AI service provider, which processes it for us to extract the account details. The findings and letters are then built from that data using fixed rules. They may still contain errors, so please review them yourself.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with companies that help us run the Service, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Hosting and database providers that store the data and run the site.
  • An AI service provider that reads uploaded reports for us.
  • A payment provider that processes payments.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect our rights or users' safety, or in connection with a sale or merger of the business. We do not share your information with credit bureaus, creditors, or collectors. Your letters are sent by you.

5. Staff access

A small number of authorized people at BlackBridge LLC can see your account and report information, for example to help with support or to confirm a payment. If a company licenses BlackBridge from us to serve its own customers, its authorized staff can see the information of the clients it serves.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and your uploaded reports at any time (see "Your choices"). Some records, such as payment confirmations, may be kept longer where the law requires.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), password protection, and access controls that keep each account's information separate. No system can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and do not share it. Only upload your credit report through our site.

8. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing BlackBridgeoffice@Gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law, and we will honor them where they apply.

9. Cookies and local storage

We keep a sign-in token in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising trackers on the Service.

10. Third-party links

The Service may link to partner sites, such as credit monitoring or credit-building products. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them. Some links may be affiliate links, and the site will say so when they are.

11. Children

The Service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you on the site or by email.

13. Contact

Privacy questions? Email BlackBridgeoffice@Gmail.com or call (669) 272-2854. BlackBridge LLC.
